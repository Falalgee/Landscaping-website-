Client Guidance & Stewardship

Frequently addressed inquiries.

Everything you need to know about navigating estate-scale landscape installations.

We handle 100% of the administrative permitting process. Our architectural division drafts compliant site plans, impervious surface coverage calculations, tree preservation affidavits, and storm drainage certifications required by local building departments and Architectural Review Boards (ARBs). We also represent your design directly during HOA committee meetings if requested.
While hardscaping, pergolas, and grading can be engineered throughout nearly any month of the year, optimal planting windows depend on specimen varieties. Autumn (September through November) is the premier season for large specimen trees and cool-season turfgrass as roots establish under warm soils and mild ambient air. Early Spring (March through May) provides an equally vibrant second window. Our horticulture team times nursery delivery to protect root balls from weather shock.
A focused courtyard or stone patio refresh is commonly completed within 2 to 3 weeks. Full estate backyard transformations (incorporating stone lounging, fire features, irrigation overhaul, and custom turf) range between 4 to 8 weeks. We deploy dedicated superintendents who stay assigned strictly to your site until substantial completion, ensuring continuous progress and clean property maintenance throughout construction.
